# Where KuroToon stands (November 2026)

An honest review of KuroToon 0.24.0 as a day-to-day reader, next to the official apps people use instead of it and
the open-source reader it came from. Competitor facts are linked to their source; everything about KuroToon was
checked on the emulator QA runs in [`.github/qa`](../.github/qa).

## The short version

KuroToon is the better reader if you already know what you want to read and where it lives. It's still the harder
app to start, because an empty app with "add a store" is a much bigger ask than WEBTOON's front page full of
covers. 0.24.0 closes most of the first-run gap (one-tap storage, a store link that accepts whatever you paste,
an empty library that points at Browse), but discovery without sources will always be KuroToon's weak spot.

### WEBTOON

WEBTOON is still the polish benchmark: instant covers, a daily schedule and a community around every series.
KuroToon matches the parts that matter while reading (a smooth long strip, Up next between episodes, the
schedule, the soundtrack) and beats it on cost, since nothing is locked behind Fast Pass. Daily Pass was dropped
in May 2025 and Ad Pass unlocks only last three days ([Chapter Brief](https://www.chapterbrief.net/blog/manhwa-platform-pricing-us-2026)).
Where WEBTOON wins: comments, creator support, recommendations that know you, and zero setup.

### Tapas

Tapas has announced it shuts down on 31 March 2027, and you lose access to unlocked episodes when it does
([Tapas](https://tapas.io/newsfeed/271)). That's the strongest argument for KuroToon's model: chapters you download
and the backups you make keep working whatever happens to a platform.

### MANGA Plus

MANGA Plus is cheap and simultaneous with Japan, and only for Shueisha titles. KuroToon can read the MANGA Plus
source through an extension, but it can't change what Shueisha makes free. For Jump readers the honest answer is
to keep MANGA Plus or MAX and use KuroToon for everything else.

## Against Mihon

KuroToon keeps Mihon's engine (sources, trackers, downloads, backups) and changes almost everything you see:
Home opens on your series, the schedule says what's out today, chapters merge across sources, universal search
shows each series once, and the reader adds episode music, ambience, saved panels and a data saver. Mihon has said
its next version drops Tachiyomi backups and Tachiyomi 1.4 extensions ([Mihon](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/releases));
KuroToon keeps both, so it's a safe landing spot for people with old backups.

Where Mihon is ahead: years of real-world use, a large community and translation team, and a proper Play-style
update channel. KuroToon's releases are now signed with one permanent key (see the README), so updates install in
place from 0.24.0 on.

## What still holds KuroToon back

1. **Getting the first source.** You have to know a store link. The add dialog now accepts any link and explains
   failures, but there's nothing to browse until a store is in.
2. **Translations.** The KuroToon screens are English first; many other languages fall back to English.
3. **No social layer.** No comments or ratings, by design, but it's the first thing WEBTOON readers miss.
4. **Mihon underneath.** Internal package names (`eu.kanade.tachiyomi`) stay for extension compatibility, and
   `mihon://` links are still accepted because store websites publish them. Neither is visible in the app.
5. **Real-device testing.** QA runs on an emulator. Please report anything that behaves differently on your phone.
