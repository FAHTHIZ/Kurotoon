# Getting started

KuroToon ships with no content. You decide where your manga comes from.

1. **Add an extension store.** Open **Me → Settings → Browse → Extension stores**, tap
   **Add extension store** and paste the URL of a store you trust (it usually ends in `index.min.json`). Links of the form
   `tachiyomi://add-repo?url=…` open KuroToon directly.
2. **Install sources.** Pick the extensions you want from the list and tap **Install**. Android will ask
   you to allow installs from KuroToon the first time.
3. **Browse and search.** Each installed source appears under **Browse → Sources**. Use **Universal
   Search** at the top to search every source at once.
4. **Build your library.** Open a title and tap **Add to library**. Library titles are checked for new
   chapters automatically (see **Settings → Library**).
5. **Read.** Tap a chapter. Tap the middle of the screen for the reader menu, where you can switch
   between paged and long-strip modes, turn on **Auto-scroll**, and change reader settings.

Everything — your library, history, downloads and settings — stays on your device. Use
**Settings → Data and storage → Create backup** to keep a copy or move to a new phone.
