# Privacy

KuroToon has no accounts and no servers.

- Your library, reading history, downloads, backups and settings are stored only on your device.
- The app talks to the websites of the sources you install, the extension repositories you add, the
  trackers you sign in to, and GitHub to check for new KuroToon releases.
- KuroToon does not include analytics or crash reporting. Crash logs are only created when you export
  them yourself.
