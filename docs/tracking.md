# Tracking

Trackers keep your reading progress in sync with services such as AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu,
MangaUpdates, Shikimori, Bangumi, MangaBaka, Comick, and self-hosted Komga, Kavita and Suwayomi.

1. Open **Settings → Tracking** and sign in to the services you use.
2. On a title's page tap **Tracking** and link the title to its entry on each service.
3. When you finish a chapter, KuroToon updates the chapter count for you. Turn this off with
   **Settings → Tracking → Update progress after reading**.

Enhanced trackers (Komga, Kavita, Suwayomi) link automatically when the title comes from the matching
source. Tracking only talks to the services you sign in to; nothing else leaves your device.
