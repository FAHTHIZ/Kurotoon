KuroToon is a free, open-source manga, manhwa and webtoon reader for Android. It is private by design: no accounts, no analytics, and your library never leaves your phone.

Features:
* Home that greets you with a featured title, popular rows from your sources and everything you're reading.
* Universal Search across every installed source at once.
* Paged and long-strip readers with auto-scroll, smooth zoom, cropping, dual pages and colour filters.
* Library with categories, unread badges, filters and automatic chapter updates.
* Downloads for offline reading, plus backups you control.
* Optional progress sync with AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, MangaUpdates and more.
* A dark crimson theme built for night reading, with Material You and pure black options.

KuroToon comes with no content and has no affiliation with any content provider. You add the sources you want to read from.
